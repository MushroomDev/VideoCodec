Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

🛡️

TL;DR — The Short Version

CodecFlow is a 100% offline macOS app that runs entirely on your device. We collect zero personal data, upload nothing, and share nothing. Every video you process in CodecFlow stays on your local disk from start to finish — it never touches any server.

1. Information We Collect

Nothing. Absolutely nothing.

CodecFlow has no data collection mechanism of any kind. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information
  • We do not collect device identifiers (UDID, IDFA, IDFV, etc.)
  • We do not collect crash reports, usage statistics, or any form of telemetry
  • We never access, read, or upload the contents of your video files
  • We have no analytics tools, tracking SDKs, or advertising frameworks

2. How Your Video Files Are Handled

All video files you import and convert in CodecFlow:

  • Always remain on your local disk — they are never uploaded to any cloud server
  • All conversion processing happens entirely on your Mac's local CPU/GPU
  • Encryption keys used by the AES feature are entered locally by you — the app never records or transmits them
  • The location of source files and output files is entirely under your control

3. Network Access

CodecFlow works without any internet connection. The app does not initiate any network requests. The only exception: if you installed CodecFlow via the Mac App Store, Apple's own receipt-validation mechanism may communicate with Apple's servers — this is a macOS system-level behavior entirely outside of CodecFlow's control.

4. Third-Party Services

CodecFlow integrates no third-party services. We do not use:

  • Third-party analytics or statistics SDKs
  • Third-party advertising platforms
  • Third-party crash-reporting services
  • Any form of backend server or cloud storage

5. Data Security

Because we collect no data, there is no data to breach or misuse. Video files encrypted with CodecFlow's AES feature are protected by your chosen key — a key we cannot access and will never attempt to recover. You are the sole custodian of your encrypted content.

6. Your Rights

Since we hold no data about you, rights such as access, correction, portability, and deletion do not apply — there is simply nothing to request. You retain full ownership and control of every file you process in CodecFlow.

7. Children's Privacy

CodecFlow does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and provides no features targeting that audience. Because no data is collected, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations impose no additional obligations on us.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy — for example, by adding a feature that requires network access — we will post the revised version on this page and provide a prominent notice within the app. Any changes take effect from the date of publication.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to reach out through the App Store developer contact page or open an issue on GitHub. We aim to respond within a few business days.
